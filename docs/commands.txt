EZVAC ALPHA 2.0 PILOT 4 - COMMAND REFERENCE
===========================================

All commands use /hvac. /climate is an alias.


BASIC COMMANDS (ezvac.use)
------------------------------

/hvac help
  Shows the commands available to the sender.

/hvac temperature
/hvac temp
  Reports local temperature, outdoor temperature, the influencing vent group,
  airflow-path distance, and core/leakage status, plus the outdoor weather and,
  in air, the humidity and "feels like" temperature. In water it reports the
  water temperature (natural water where no pool system reaches) and the air
  above.

/hvac hud [on|off]
  Shows or hides the on-screen climate HUD for yourself (ezvac.hud). With no
  argument it toggles. The choice is remembered across restarts. The style
  (sidebar, action bar, or boss bar) is set by hud.style in config.yml.

/hvac list
  Lists HVAC systems in the player's current world with mode, room/target
  temperature, and running/registered equipment counts.

/hvac info
  Looks up the targeted thermostat, equipment unit, vent, thermometer, RPM
  monitor, settings panel, or dehumidifier within six blocks. A thermostat also
  shows its room's humidity; a dehumidifier shows the space it dries, its
  humidity, setpoint, state and tank.


CREATE DEVICES (ezvac.admin)
--------------------------------

/hvac create thermostat <id> <group> [targetF]
  Look at a sign. Creates the only thermostat/controller for that group in the
  sign's world. IDs and group labels are 1-32 letters, numbers, '_' or '-'.

/hvac create heatpump <group>
/hvac create ac <group>
/hvac create furnace <group>

/hvac create poolthermostat <id> <group> [targetF]
  Registers the targeted sign as a pool thermostat: identical to a thermostat
  except that it clamps to the water target range (75-104 F by default) and may
  only control a system powered by water-loop equipment (boilers and pool heat
  pumps). Defaults to 82 F.
  "pool" works as a shorthand for "poolthermostat".

/hvac create boiler <group>
  Registers the targeted dispenser as a boiler: a heating-only water-loop
  source for a system whose vents are waxed copper grates placed in water.

/hvac create poolheatpump <group>
  Registers the targeted dispenser as a pool heat pump: a variable-speed
  water-loop source that heats and cools, so a pool can be cooled as well as
  heated. It can share a system with a boiler. "poolhp" is a shorthand.
  Look at a dispenser. Registers typed equipment in the specified group.

/hvac create thermometer [label]
/hvac create hygrometer [label]
/hvac create thermohygrometer [label]
  Look at a sign. Creates an independent point sensor at that block. It needs no
  group and may be placed outdoors; its reading matches /hvac temperature there.
  A thermometer shows temperature, a hygrometer humidity (inside and outside),
  and a thermo-hygrometer temperature, humidity and feels-like.
  "thermohygro" is a shorthand.

/hvac create rpmmonitor <group> <heatpump|ac|furnace|boiler|poolheatpump> [label]
  Look at a sign. Matching typed equipment must already exist in the group.

/hvac create settingspanel <group> [label]
  Look at a sign. Creates the group's single operating-profile panel in NORMAL.
  The group must already contain a thermostat or equipment.

/hvac create dehumidifier [label]
  Look at a sign on an iron block (on its side, or standing on top) that sits on
  a waxed copper grate. Registers a standalone dehumidifier at 45%. It needs no
  group. The grate must not be linked as a water vent. "dehum" is a shorthand.

/hvac create humidifier [label]
  Look at a sign on the side of an iron block that has a waxed cut copper slab
  (bottom half) on top. Registers a standalone, plumbed humidifier at 40%. It
  needs no group. "hum" is a shorthand.

/hvac create sensor [label]
  Look at a stone button (temperature), polished blackstone button (humidity)
  or mangrove button (both). The button decides what the sensor measures.

/hvac create sensor-hub [label]
  Look at a sign. A sensor hub shows up to nine sensors, three to a page.
  "sensorhub" is a shorthand.

/hvac sensor-tool
  Gives the HVAC Sensor Link Tool (it glints, to tell it from the duct tool).
  Right-click a sensor hub to select it, then right-click sensors to link them
  (at most nine, same world). Sneak-right-click a sensor to unlink it.

SENSOR HUB USE (ezvac.use)
--------------------------

- Right-click a hub to turn the page.
- Sneak-right-click it to switch auto-flip on or off (every 5 seconds).
- Right-click a sensor to see its reading in chat. Sensors never send redstone.

DEHUMIDIFIER AND HUMIDIFIER USE (ezvac.use)
--------------------------------------------

- Right-click the sign to step the setpoint: 35, 40, 45, 50, 55, 60% (wraps).
- Sneak-right-click to switch it on or off. Off freezes the tank as it is.
- It starts 2% above the setpoint and runs until the setpoint is reached.
- A full tank waterlogs the grate and shows "Emptying..." for 30 seconds.
- Breaking the sign, the iron block or the grate removes it.
- A humidifier is used the same way: its setpoints are 30, 35, 40, 45, 50%, it
  starts 2% below the setpoint and runs up to it, and while it runs its slab
  holds water and bubbles. It never needs refilling. Breaking the sign, the
  iron block or the slab removes it.

SETTINGS PANEL USE (ezvac.use)
--------------------------------

- Right-click advances NORMAL -> TURBO -> ECO -> NORMAL.
- Sneak-right-click moves in the opposite direction.
- ECO defaults to a 2,400 RPM cap, NORMAL to 3,600, and TURBO to 4,200.
- Profiles affect every heat pump and AC in the group in sync.
- Furnaces remain fixed at full heating capacity in every profile.
- Removing or losing the panel returns the group to NORMAL automatically.


THERMOSTATS (ezvac.admin)
-----------------------------

/hvac thermostat <id> set <temperatureF>
  Sets and safely clamps the target to the configured target range.

/hvac thermostat <id> enable
/hvac thermostat <id> disable
  Enables or fails the thermostat safe to idle.

/hvac thermostat <id> group <group>
  Moves the thermostat to another group in the same world. The destination may
  not already have a thermostat.

/hvac thermostat <id> info
  Shows room/target, mode, group, and enable state.

Thermostat signs may also be edited. Put a finite number on line 3. Non-admin
players cannot modify registered thermostat signs. Sneak-right-clicking a
thermostat sign (ezvac.use) switches its system off or back on, like
/hvac thermostat <id> disable|enable; units disabled on their own stay off; thermometer, RPM, and
settings-panel and dehumidifier signs are always read-only through the sign
editor.


EQUIPMENT AND GROUPS (ezvac.admin)
--------------------------------------

/hvac equipment enable
/hvac equipment disable
/hvac equipment group <group>
/hvac equipment info
  Look at registered equipment. Group changes first stop and clean the old
  output; the change is refused while cleanup is waiting on an unloaded chunk.

/hvac group <group> enable
/hvac group <group> disable
/hvac group <group> info
  Controls or inspects the named group in the player's current world.


VENT LINKING (ezvac.admin)
------------------------------

/hvac duct-tool
  Gives the persistent HVAC Duct Link Tool. "/hvac tool" still works.

1. Right-click a registered dispenser to select its group.
2. Right-click vent blocks in the same world to link them as vents:
     - iron trapdoor      -> air vent, conditions the room around it
     - waxed copper grate -> water vent, conditions the water around it
3. Sneak-right-click a registered vent to unlink it.

The selected group must still contain equipment when a vent is linked. A
dehumidifier's grate cannot be linked as a vent.

A system is all air or all water, and its equipment must match. Linking a
waxed copper grate to a system that uses iron trapdoors is refused, as is
linking either kind to a system whose equipment belongs to the other loop:
boilers and pool heat pumps are the water loop, and heat pumps, air
conditioners, and furnaces are the air loop.


REMOVAL AND DIAGNOSTICS (ezvac.admin)
-----------------------------------------

/hvac remove
  Removes the targeted registered device. Equipment retracts its owned output,
  or persists cleanup until the output chunk loads. Removing a settings panel
  returns its HVAC group to NORMAL.

/hvac stats
  Reports device totals and airflow cache/rebuild/cap statistics.

/hvac sync
  Queues one coalesced zero-time authoritative reconciliation. This changes no
  simulation time; it immediately applies command/block/world state changes.


INSTALL / UPGRADE
-----------------

1. Stop the server and back up the existing plugins/EZvac directory, if present.
2. Put EzVac-ALPHA-2.0-P4.jar in the server plugins directory. Upgrading from
   1.x moves hvac.yml to format 6 and keeps the original as
   hvac.yml.format5.bak.
3. Start Paper 1.21.1 (or Purpur) on Java 21.
4. Confirm that EZvac enables without errors.
5. Use /hvac stats and /hvac list, then test one full heating and cooling cycle.
